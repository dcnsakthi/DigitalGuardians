About Digital Guardians

Digital Guardians aligns with the Digital for Life Fund's focus on Digital Safety and Security by promoting personal responsibility for online safety, empowering children and youth to navigate online spaces safely, and supporting parents with actionable skills to guide their children's digital journey.

🎯 Project Objectives

  • Raise awareness about online misinformation, scams, and deepfakes among the general public
  • Equip children and youth with critical thinking and digital literacy skills
  • Support parents and caregivers with practical tools and workshops

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Target Audience

  • Primary: Children and youth (ages 10–18)
  • Secondary: Parents, guardians, and educators
