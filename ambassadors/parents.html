Make a Difference in Your Community

As a Digital Guardians Parent Ambassador, you'll use your experience as a parent to help other families create safer online environments for their children. You don't need to be a tech expert—just a caring parent who wants to make a positive impact.

Ambassador Roles

Choose the role that best fits your interests, skills, and availability:

Workshop Facilitator

What You'll Do:

  • Lead parent education workshops at schools or community centers
  • Co-facilitate family digital safety sessions
  • Present at PTA/PTO meetings and school events

Time Commitment: 4-6 hours per month

Training Required: 12 hours initial + ongoing support

Peer Mentor

What You'll Do:

  • Provide one-on-one support to parents with questions
  • Facilitate parent discussion groups (online or in-person)
  • Offer guidance on specific digital safety challenges

Time Commitment: 3-5 hours per month (flexible)

Training Required: 8 hours initial + peer support

Community Advocate

What You'll Do:

  • Raise awareness about digital safety in your community
  • Organize local events and information sessions
  • Connect families with resources and support

Time Commitment: 2-4 hours per month

Training Required: 6 hours initial + resource orientation

Content Creator

What You'll Do:

  • Write blog posts or articles sharing experiences and tips
  • Create social media content for parent audiences
  • Develop practical resources and guides

Time Commitment: Flexible (2-8 hours per month)

Training Required: 4 hours initial + editorial support

Resource Coordinator

What You'll Do:

  • Maintain and distribute digital safety resource materials
  • Coordinate volunteer schedules and activities
  • Support other ambassadors with logistics

Time Commitment: 3-5 hours per month

Training Required: 4 hours initial + administrative support

Training Program

We provide comprehensive training to prepare you for success as a Parent Ambassador. No technical expertise required—we'll teach you everything you need to know!

Core Training Modules

  • Module 1: Digital Safety Fundamentals (3 hours)
  • Module 2: Age-Appropriate Guidance (2 hours)
  • Module 3: Communication Skills for Parents (2 hours)
  • Module 4: Current Trends and Challenges (2 hours)
  • Module 5: Role-Specific Skills (2-4 hours based on role)
  • Module 6: Resources and Support Systems (1 hour)

Training Format

  • Self-Paced Online Modules: Complete at your convenience
  • Live Virtual Sessions: Interactive training with instructors (optional but recommended)
  • Practice Opportunities: Shadow experienced ambassadors
  • Ongoing Education: Monthly webinars on emerging topics
  • Peer Learning: Regular ambassador meetups and discussions

Requirements

  • Age: Must be 18 or older
  • Parenting Experience: Parent, guardian, or caregiver of children/teens
  • Commitment: Minimum 6-month commitment preferred
  • Background Check: Required for roles involving direct child interaction
  • Training: Complete required training modules
  • Attitude: Non-judgmental, supportive approach to helping families
  • Technology: Basic computer/smartphone skills and internet access

Getting Started

  1. Submit Application: Complete our brief online application (10 minutes)
  2. Orientation Session: Attend a virtual information session (1 hour)
  3. Background Check: Complete required screening (if applicable)
  4. Training: Complete core training modules at your pace
  5. Role Selection: Choose your preferred ambassador role(s)
  6. Mentorship: Paired with an experienced ambassador for support
  7. Launch: Begin your ambassador activities!

Rolling Admissions:

We accept new parent ambassadors year-round! Start your training as soon as your application is approved.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be a tech expert?

Not at all! We provide all the training you need. Your experience as a parent is what matters most.

How much time do I need to commit?

It varies by role, from 2-8 hours per month. You can adjust your involvement based on your schedule.

Is there any cost to join?

No! The program is completely free. We provide all training and materials at no cost to you.

Can I volunteer remotely?

Yes! Many ambassador activities can be done virtually, making it accessible regardless of location.

What if I need to take a break?

We understand that life gets busy. You can take breaks as needed and return when you're ready.
