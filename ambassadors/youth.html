Youth Leaders Program

Empower young people to become digital safety champions among their peers

Why Become a Youth Leader?

As a Digital Guardians Youth Leader, you'll gain valuable leadership skills while making a real difference in your community. Young people often learn best from their peers, and your voice can reach those who might not listen to adults.

Leadership Development

Build public speaking, teaching, and mentoring skills that will serve you throughout life

Official Certification

Earn recognized credentials that enhance college applications and resumes

Community Impact

Make a real difference by educating peers about digital safety

National Network

Connect with other youth leaders across the country

Program Requirements

Age: 14-19 years old
Commitment: 20 hours initial training + 5 hours/month
Interest: Passion for digital safety and helping others
Skills: Good communication (we'll help you improve!)
Support: Parent/guardian approval
Completion: All training modules and final assessment

Training Journey

Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1-4)

Digital Safety Fundamentals

  • Comprehensive overview of digital safety topics
  • Understanding online threats and risks
  • Privacy protection and security best practices
  • Social media safety and digital citizenship

Phase 2: Leadership Skills (Weeks 5-8)

Teaching and Communication

  • Public speaking and presentation skills
  • Peer education techniques
  • Active listening and empathy building
  • Conflict resolution and difficult conversations

Phase 3: Practical Application (Weeks 9-12)

Hands-On Experience

  • Developing workshop content for different age groups
  • Creating engaging presentations and activities
  • Observing experienced ambassadors
  • Co-facilitating practice sessions

Phase 4: Certification (Weeks 13-16)

Assessment and Launch

  • Knowledge assessment
  • Facilitation demonstration
  • Peer and mentor feedback
  • Official certification ceremony

What You'll Learn

Core Topics

  • Identifying fake news and misinformation
  • Recognizing phishing and scams
  • Understanding deepfakes
  • Password security and account protection
  • Privacy settings and data protection
  • Cyberbullying prevention and response

Teaching Skills

  • Lesson planning and content development
  • Engaging presentation techniques
  • Facilitating group discussions
  • Using interactive activities and games
  • Adapting content for different ages
  • Handling challenging questions

Leadership Competencies

  • Effective communication
  • Building trust and rapport
  • Mentoring and peer support
  • Project management basics
  • Team collaboration
  • Self-reflection and continuous improvement

Ambassador Activities

As a certified Youth Leader, you can participate in various activities:

  • School Presentations: Lead workshops in your own or partner schools
  • Community Events: Staff information booths at local events
  • Online Content: Create educational content for social media
  • Peer Mentoring: One-on-one support for students with questions
  • Parent Workshops: Co-facilitate family education sessions
  • New Ambassador Training: Help train the next cohort of leaders
  • Advocacy Projects: Develop campaigns for specific safety issues

  3. Interview: Participate in a brief virtual interview (30 minutes)
  4. Selection: Applications reviewed within 2 weeks
