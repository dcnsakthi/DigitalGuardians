# Digital Guardians
## 🛡️ Digital Guardians: Empowering Families for a Safer Online Future!

This project aligns with the Digital for Life Fund's focus on Digital Safety and Security by promoting personal responsibility for online safety, empowering children and youth to navigate online spaces safely, and supporting parents with actionable skills to guide their children’s digital journey. It also supports inclusivity through multilingual resources and community ambassador training, fostering a safer and more digitally literate Singaporean community.

### 🎯 Project Objectives

- Raise awareness about online misinformation, scams, and deepfakes among the general public.
- Equip children and youth with critical thinking and digital literacy skills to navigate online spaces safely.
- Support parents and caregivers with practical tools and workshops to guide their children’s digital behavior.

### 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Target Audience
- Primary: Children and youth (ages 10–18)
- Secondary: Parents, guardians, and educators

### 🧩 Key Components
#### Interactive Workshops
- Age-appropriate sessions on identifying fake news, phishing, and deepfakes.
- Parent-child joint sessions to foster open digital communication.
#### Digital Safety Toolkit
- Printable guides, checklists, and conversation starters for families.
- Multilingual resources to ensure inclusivity.
#### Gamified Learning App or Web Portal
- Quizzes, simulations, and role-play scenarios to reinforce safe online behavior.
#### Community Ambassadors
- Train youth leaders and parent volunteers to become digital safety advocates in schools and neighborhoods.
