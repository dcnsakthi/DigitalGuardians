# Community Ambassadors Program

## Overview
The Community Ambassadors Program empowers youth leaders and parent volunteers to become digital safety advocates in schools and neighborhoods. This initiative creates a network of trained ambassadors who can educate families and communities about online safety, privacy, and responsible digital citizenship.

## Mission
To build a grassroots movement of digital safety advocates who can reach families at the local level, making digital safety education accessible and practical for everyone.

## Program Structure

### Ambassador Roles

#### Youth Leaders (Ages 14-18)
Youth ambassadors serve as peer educators, bringing digital safety awareness to their schools and youth organizations.

**Responsibilities:**
- Lead peer workshops and presentations
- Create and share digital safety content on social media
- Organize school campaigns and awareness events
- Mentor younger students on safe online practices
- Provide feedback on youth-relevant digital safety topics

#### Parent Volunteers
Parent ambassadors educate other parents and facilitate community conversations about digital parenting.

**Responsibilities:**
- Host parent education sessions and workshops
- Lead neighborhood digital safety discussion groups
- Provide resources and support to families
- Coordinate with schools and community organizations
- Share practical tips and experiences with other parents

## Training Curriculum

### Core Modules (All Ambassadors)

#### Module 1: Digital Safety Fundamentals (2 hours)
- Understanding online risks and threats
- Privacy basics and data protection
- Recognizing cyberbullying and online harassment
- Safe social media practices
- Digital footprint awareness

#### Module 2: Effective Communication (2 hours)
- Public speaking basics
- Facilitation skills for group discussions
- Creating engaging presentations
- Active listening and empathy
- Handling difficult questions

#### Module 3: Educational Resources and Tools (1.5 hours)
- Navigating DigitalGuardians resources
- Using educational materials effectively
- Creating age-appropriate content
- Leveraging technology for outreach
- Resource sharing best practices

#### Module 4: Community Engagement (1.5 hours)
- Building partnerships with schools and organizations
- Organizing events and workshops
- Promoting digital safety initiatives
- Measuring impact and gathering feedback
- Sustaining long-term engagement

### Youth Leader Specialization (3 hours)

#### Module 5: Peer Education Strategies
- Understanding peer influence and dynamics
- Creating relatable content for youth audiences
- Social media advocacy techniques
- Gaming safety and online communities
- Mental health and screen time balance

#### Module 6: School-Based Advocacy
- Working with school administrators and teachers
- Planning school-wide campaigns
- Integrating digital safety into student activities
- Student club leadership
- Peer mentorship programs

### Parent Volunteer Specialization (3 hours)

#### Module 7: Digital Parenting Essentials
- Age-appropriate guidelines for device use
- Parental controls and monitoring tools
- Building trust and open communication with children
- Managing family screen time
- Responding to online incidents

#### Module 8: Community Organization
- Facilitating parent support groups
- Hosting neighborhood workshops
- Partnering with PTAs and parent organizations
- Creating sustainable community programs
- Sharing resources among families

## Ambassador Certification

### Requirements
- Complete all core training modules
- Complete role-specific specialization modules
- Participate in a practice presentation or workshop
- Commit to a minimum of 10 hours of service in the first 6 months
- Agree to the Ambassador Code of Conduct

### Certification Benefits
- Official DigitalGuardians Ambassador certificate
- Access to exclusive resources and materials
- Ongoing training and professional development
- Community of practice and peer support
- Recognition on the DigitalGuardians website

## Getting Started

### Application Process
1. Complete online application form
2. Participate in informational session
3. Submit references (2 for youth leaders, 1 for parents)
4. Complete background check (if required by local regulations)
5. Attend orientation session

### Training Schedule
- Self-paced online modules available year-round
- Live virtual training sessions offered monthly
- In-person regional training events quarterly
- Flexible scheduling to accommodate school and work commitments

### Time Commitment
- Initial training: 8-10 hours over 2-4 weeks
- Ongoing service: Minimum 2 hours per month
- Continued education: 4-6 hours per year

## Impact Tracking

Ambassadors are encouraged to track and report their activities:
- Number of presentations and workshops delivered
- Estimated audience reach
- Materials distributed
- Partnerships formed
- Success stories and testimonials

## Ambassador Code of Conduct

All ambassadors must:
- Act with integrity and represent DigitalGuardians values
- Respect privacy and confidentiality
- Provide accurate, evidence-based information
- Be inclusive and non-judgmental
- Maintain appropriate boundaries with participants
- Report any concerns or incidents to program coordinators
- Commit to continuous learning and improvement

## Recognition and Growth

### Ambassador Levels
- **Certified Ambassador**: Completed training and basic service requirements
- **Lead Ambassador**: 50+ hours of service and mentoring new ambassadors
- **Master Ambassador**: 100+ hours of service and leadership in program development

### Opportunities for Advancement
- Regional coordinator positions
- Content creation and curriculum development
- Speaking at conferences and events
- Contributing to DigitalGuardians blog and resources
- Participating in research and program evaluation
