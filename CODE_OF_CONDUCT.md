# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Digital Guardians is committed to providing a safe, welcoming, and inclusive environment for everyone involved in creating a safer digital future for families in Singapore. We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

### Expected Behavior

Participants in the Digital Guardians community are expected to:

✅ **Be Respectful**
- Treat all community members with respect and dignity
- Use welcoming and inclusive language
- Be considerate of different viewpoints and experiences
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully

✅ **Be Supportive**
- Offer help and guidance to newcomers
- Share knowledge and resources generously
- Celebrate others' contributions and successes
- Provide constructive, helpful feedback

✅ **Be Professional**
- Focus on what is best for the community
- Maintain confidentiality when handling sensitive information
- Give credit where credit is due
- Act with integrity in all interactions

✅ **Be Child-Safe**
- Never share content that could harm children
- Report any concerns about child safety immediately
- Follow safeguarding best practices
- Prioritize child protection in all decisions

✅ **Be Inclusive**
- Welcome people from all backgrounds
- Consider accessibility in communications
- Respect cultural differences
- Use inclusive language and examples

### Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated:

❌ **Harassment**
- Offensive comments related to personal characteristics
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Deliberate intimidation or stalking
- Inappropriate physical contact

❌ **Harmful Content**
- Content that could harm children or vulnerable persons
- Sharing of illegal material
- Promotion of dangerous activities
- Sharing personal information without consent

❌ **Disruptive Behavior**
- Trolling or insulting comments
- Personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Sustained disruption of discussions

❌ **Dishonest Conduct**
- Plagiarism or misattribution
- Spreading misinformation
- Impersonation of others
- Abuse of trust or position

## Child Safeguarding

Given our focus on protecting children, we have additional safeguarding requirements:

### Mandatory Reporting
All community members must immediately report:
- Suspected child abuse or neglect
- Content depicting harm to children
- Inappropriate contact with minors
- Violations of child safety policies

### Working with Minors
When contributing content for or working with children:
- Obtain appropriate permissions and consent
- Never include identifying information of minors
- Use age-appropriate language and examples
- Follow best practices for child protection

### Content Guidelines
All content must:
- Be age-appropriate for the intended audience
- Not include graphic or traumatic material without warnings
- Respect children's privacy and dignity
- Promote positive digital citizenship

## Enforcement

### Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:

1. **For Urgent Safety Issues**
   - Contact local authorities immediately
   - Report to: safety@digitalguardians.sg

2. **For Code of Conduct Violations**
   - Email: conduct@digitalguardians.sg
   - Include date, time, description, and any evidence
   - Reports are kept confidential

3. **Anonymous Reporting**
   - Use our anonymous reporting form: [Link]
   - Still include as much detail as possible

### Review Process

1. **Receipt** (Within 24 hours)
   - Acknowledgment of report received
   - Initial assessment of severity

2. **Investigation** (1-7 days)
   - Gather information from all parties
   - Review evidence and context
   - Determine if Code of Conduct was violated

3. **Resolution** (As appropriate)
   - Determine appropriate response
   - Communicate decision to relevant parties
   - Implement consequences if needed

### Consequences

Depending on the severity and circumstances, consequences may include:

**Level 1: Warning**
- First-time, minor infractions
- Written warning explaining violation
- Guidance on expected behavior

**Level 2: Temporary Suspension**
- Repeated minor infractions or moderate violation
- Temporary removal from community activities
- Required acknowledgment of expectations before return

**Level 3: Permanent Ban**
- Serious violations
- Repeated violations after warnings
- Permanent removal from all community spaces
- May involve reporting to authorities

**Immediate Removal**
- Any behavior that poses immediate risk to children
- Illegal activities
- Serious harassment or threats
- Sharing of illegal content

## Appeals

If you believe a decision was made in error:
1. Submit written appeal within 14 days
2. Provide additional context or evidence
3. Appeals reviewed by different team members
4. Decision communicated within 7 days
