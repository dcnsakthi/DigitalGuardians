Translation Support

We welcome community contributions to expand our language offerings. If you're interested in helping translate Digital Guardians resources into additional languages, we'd love to hear from you.

  • Volunteer Translators: Native speakers passionate about digital safety
  • Professional Review: All translations undergo quality assurance
  • Cultural Adaptation: Content adapted to be culturally relevant
  • Continuous Updates: Translations maintained as content evolves

Language Notes

  • All materials are professionally translated by native speakers
  • Content is culturally adapted while maintaining core safety principles
  • New resources are added regularly - check back for updates
  • Workshop materials available in all supported languages
  • Video content includes subtitles in multiple languages

Frequently Asked Questions

Are all resources available in every language?

We strive to provide complete coverage in all supported languages. Some newly added content may take time to be translated, but core resources are available in all listed languages.

Can I request a specific language?

Yes! We prioritize languages based on community need. Contact us with your request and we'll consider it for future expansion.

Are workshops available in multiple languages?

Yes, our interactive workshops can be conducted in any of the supported languages. Workshop materials and facilitator guides are fully translated.

How do I switch the website language?

Use the language selector in the navigation menu to change the entire website interface to your preferred language.
