## Program Information

### Structure and Requirements

**From [Community Ambassadors Program Guide](../COMMUNITY_AMBASSADORS.md):**

#### Ambassador Roles
- **Youth Leaders (14-18)**: Peer educators in schools and youth organizations
- **Parent Volunteers**: Parent educators facilitating community conversations

#### Training Curriculum
**Core Modules (7 hours total):**
1. Digital Safety Fundamentals (2 hours)
2. Effective Communication (2 hours)
3. Educational Resources and Tools (1.5 hours)
4. Community Engagement (1.5 hours)

**Youth Leader Specialization (3 hours):**
5. Peer Education Strategies
6. School-Based Advocacy

**Parent Volunteer Specialization (3 hours):**
7. Digital Parenting Essentials
8. Community Organization

#### Certification Requirements
- Complete all core training modules
- Complete role-specific specialization modules
- Participate in practice presentation/workshop
- Commit to 10+ hours service in first 6 months
- Agree to Ambassador Code of Conduct

#### Ambassador Levels
- **Certified Ambassador**: Training complete, basic service requirements met
- **Lead Ambassador**: 50+ hours service, mentoring new ambassadors
- **Master Ambassador**: 100+ hours service, program development leadership

### For Different Purposes

#### Starting as a New Ambassador
1. Read [README](../README.md) (5 min)

2. Use [Workshop Planning Template](templates/workshop-planning-template.md)

1. Use [Activity Tracker](templates/activity-tracker.md) consistently
2. Reference [Digital Safety Resources](resources/digital-safety-resources.md) for content
3. Apply strategies from role-specific guide
