## 🆘 Emergency Contacts Quick Reference

### United States
- **Emergency Services**: 911
- **Suicide Prevention Lifeline**: 988
- **Crisis Text Line**: Text HOME to 741741
- **NCMEC CyberTipline**: 1-800-843-5678 or [CyberTipline.org](https://www.cybertipline.org/)
- **FBI IC3**: [IC3.gov](https://www.ic3.gov/)

### International Resources
- **International Child Helpline**: [childhelplineinternational.org](https://www.childhelplineinternational.org/)

## 💡 Tips for Using This Toolkit

### For Maximum Effectiveness
1. **Start Small**: Don't try to read everything at once
2. **Focus on What's Relevant**: Use the use-case guide above
3. **Print Key Resources**: Keep physical copies accessible
4. **Review Regularly**: Digital safety is ongoing, not one-time
5. **Involve Everyone**: Share appropriate resources with all family members
6. **Customize**: Adapt these resources to your family's needs
7. **Stay Current**: Check back for updates

### Making It Manageable
- **Week 1**: Read the relevant guide for your situation
- **Week 2**: Implement one checklist
- **Week 3**: Have your first family discussion
- **Week 4**: Review and adjust
- **Ongoing**: Monthly check-ins and updates
