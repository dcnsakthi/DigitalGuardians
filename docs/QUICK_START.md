# Ambassador Quick Start Guide

Welcome to the Community Ambassadors Program! This quick start guide will help you get oriented and begin your journey as a digital safety advocate.

---

## Your First Steps

### Week 1: Orientation
- [ ] Read the [Community Ambassadors Program Overview](../COMMUNITY_AMBASSADORS.md)
- [ ] Review your role-specific guide:
  - Youth Leaders: [Youth Leader Ambassador Guide](training/youth-leader-guide.md)
  - Parents: [Parent Volunteer Ambassador Guide](training/parent-volunteer-guide.md)
- [ ] Join the Ambassador online community
- [ ] Introduce yourself to your regional coordinator

### Week 2-4: Training
- [ ] Complete Core Module 1: Digital Safety Fundamentals (2 hours)
- [ ] Complete Core Module 2: Effective Communication (2 hours)
- [ ] Complete Core Module 3: Educational Resources and Tools (1.5 hours)
- [ ] Complete Core Module 4: Community Engagement (1.5 hours)
- [ ] Complete your role-specific specialization modules (3 hours)
- [ ] Participate in practice presentation or workshop

### Week 5+: Launch
- [ ] Plan your first activity using the [Workshop Planning Template](templates/workshop-planning-template.md)
- [ ] Review [Digital Safety Resources](resources/digital-safety-resources.md) for content ideas
- [ ] Set up your [Activity Tracker](templates/activity-tracker.md)
- [ ] Host your first workshop or create your first content
- [ ] Report your activity and celebrate your success!

1. **Digital Safety Fundamentals** (2 hours)

## Time Commitments

### Initial Training
- **8-10 hours** over 2-4 weeks
- Self-paced online modules
- Live virtual training sessions available monthly
- Flexible scheduling

### Ongoing Service
- **Minimum 2 hours per month**
- Workshops, presentations, discussion groups
- Social media content creation
- Community events

### Continued Education
- **4-6 hours per year**
- Monthly webinars on emerging topics
- Quarterly skill-building workshops
- Annual ambassador conference

## Common Questions

**Q: How much time will this take?**
A: Initial training is 8-10 hours over 2-4 weeks. Ongoing commitment is minimum 2 hours per month. It's flexible to fit your schedule!

**Q: Do I need to be a tech expert?**
A: No! You'll learn what you need in training. Focus on teaching principles and connecting people to resources.

**Q: What if I make a mistake?**
A: It's okay! We all make mistakes. Be honest, correct information, and keep learning.

**Q: Can I do this with limited time?**
A: Absolutely! Start small. Even one workshop or social media post makes a difference.

**Q: How do I handle sensitive topics?**
A: Training will prepare you. Always refer serious issues to professionals. You're an educator, not a counselor.

**Q: Will I get support?**
A: Yes! You'll have access to coordinators, resources, peer community, and ongoing training.
