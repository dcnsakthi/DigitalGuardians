## Tips for Success

### Creating Your Agreement
1. **Involve everyone**: Children are more likely to follow rules they helped create
2. **Be specific**: Vague rules are hard to enforce
3. **Be realistic**: Don't set rules you can't or won't enforce
4. **Be flexible**: Be willing to adjust as you learn what works
5. **Focus on values**: Explain why rules exist, not just what they are

### Implementing Your Agreement
1. **Start gradually**: Don't change everything at once
2. **Be consistent**: Apply rules fairly to everyone
3. **Follow up**: Check in regularly on how it's working
4. **Praise success**: Acknowledge when rules are followed
5. **Address violations calmly**: Use them as teaching opportunities

### Making It Stick
1. **Post it visibly**: Put the agreement where everyone can see it
2. **Reference it regularly**: Remind family members as needed
3. **Model it**: Parents must follow rules too
4. **Update it**: Keep it current as children grow
5. **Celebrate milestones**: Acknowledge good digital citizenship
