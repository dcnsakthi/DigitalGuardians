# Kid's Guide to Staying Safe Online

## Hey There, Digital Explorer! 🌟

This guide will help you have fun online while staying safe. Think of it as your digital safety superhero guide!

---

## The Golden Rules of Internet Safety

### Rule #1: Keep Your Secrets Secret 🔒
**Never share:**
- Your full name
- Your address or school name
- Your phone number
- Your passwords (except with parents)
- Your schedule or where you'll be
- Photos that show where you live

**Why?** Bad people can use this information to find you or trick you.

### Rule #2: Think Before You Click 🤔
- Don't download things without asking
- Don't click on pop-ups or weird links
- If something seems too good to be true, it probably is
- Ask an adult if you're not sure

### Rule #3: Be Kind Online 💖
- Treat others how you want to be treated
- Don't say things online you wouldn't say in person
- Stand up for others who are being bullied
- Use nice words and emojis

### Rule #4: Tell a Trusted Adult 🗣️
**Always tell a parent, teacher, or trusted adult if:**
- Someone makes you feel uncomfortable
- Someone asks you to keep secrets
- Someone asks for personal information
- You see something that scares or confuses you
- Someone is mean to you or your friends

### Rule #5: Use Strong Passwords 🔐
- Make passwords long and unique
- Use a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols
- Don't use your name or birthday
- Don't share passwords with friends
- Example: "BlueDinosaur#2025!" instead of "password123"

## Social Media Smarts

### Before You Post, Ask Yourself:
- [ ] Would I want my grandma to see this?
- [ ] Could this hurt someone's feelings?
- [ ] Will I still want this online in 5 years?
- [ ] Does this reveal personal information?
- [ ] Am I posting this because I want to or because I feel pressured?

### Privacy Settings Checklist
- [ ] Profile is set to private
- [ ] Only accept friend requests from people you know in real life
- [ ] Location services are turned off
- [ ] Posts are only visible to friends
- [ ] Comments from strangers are disabled

---

## Gaming Safety Tips 🎮

### Playing It Safe
- Only play with people you know in real life
- Don't share your real name - use a gaming username
- Turn off voice chat with strangers
- Report players who are mean or inappropriate
- Take breaks every hour
- Don't make real-world plans with online gaming friends

### Red Flags in Gaming
🚩 Someone wants to move the chat to a different app
🚩 Someone asks for personal information
🚩 Someone sends you gifts or offers
🚩 Someone wants to video chat
🚩 Someone asks you to keep your friendship secret

## Video Chat Safety 📹

### Before You Video Chat:
- [ ] Ask parent permission
- [ ] Only chat with people you know
- [ ] Check what's visible in your background
- [ ] Wear appropriate clothing
- [ ] Never show personal information on screen

## What to Do If Something Goes Wrong

### If Someone Is Being Mean (Cyberbullying)
1. **Don't respond** - it makes it worse
2. **Save the evidence** - take screenshots
3. **Block the person**
4. **Tell an adult** immediately
5. **Report** to the app or website

### If Someone Makes You Uncomfortable
1. **Stop talking** to them right away
2. **Don't delete** the messages (they're evidence)
3. **Tell a parent** or trusted adult
4. **Block** the person
5. You did **nothing wrong** - it's okay to tell

### If You See Something Scary or Inappropriate
1. **Close** the page or app
2. **Don't investigate** further
3. **Tell an adult** right away
4. **Don't blame yourself** - it happens

## Fun Ways to Stay Smart Online 🌈

### The SMART Acronym
- **S**afe: Keep personal information private
- **M**eet: Never meet online friends in real life
- **A**ccepting: Don't accept files, friend requests from strangers
- **R**eliable: Not everything online is true - check with an adult
- **T**ell: Tell an adult if something makes you uncomfortable

### Quick Quiz - Are You Cyber Smart?
1. Someone you don't know wants to be your friend online. What do you do?
   - ✅ Ignore or decline the request
   - ❌ Accept because it's nice to make new friends

2. You see a cool game that says "Download Now for Free Prizes!" What do you do?
   - ✅ Ask a parent first
   - ❌ Download it right away

3. Someone in a game asks where you go to school. What do you do?
   - ✅ Don't tell them and tell a parent
   - ❌ Tell them because they seem nice

## Your Digital Footprint 👣

Everything you do online leaves a "footprint" - a trail that doesn't go away!

**Good digital footprints:**
- Nice comments
- Cool art you create
- Helping others
- Positive posts

**Bad digital footprints:**
- Mean comments
- Embarrassing photos
- Sharing private information
- Lying or cheating

**Remember:** Future you (and future teachers, coaches, or employers!) might see what you post today!

## Screen Time Balance ⏰

### Healthy Tech Habits
- Take a 5-minute break every hour
- No screens 1 hour before bedtime
- Do other fun activities (sports, reading, art, playing outside)
- Follow your family's screen time rules
- Listen to your body - if your eyes hurt or you feel cranky, take a break

---

## Emergency Help 🆘

### If you need help right now:
- Tell a parent or trusted adult
- Call 911 if you're in danger
- Text HOME to 741741 (Crisis Text Line) if you need to talk
- Call 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline)

### Remember: It's NEVER your fault if something bad happens online. Adults are here to help, not to punish you!
