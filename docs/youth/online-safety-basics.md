# Online Safety Basics for Youth

Welcome! This guide is for YOU - to help you stay safe, have fun, and make the most of your time online.

## Why This Matters

You're part of the first generation growing up truly digital. The internet is an amazing tool for learning, creating, connecting with friends, and expressing yourself. But just like in the real world, there are some risks online too. The good news? You can learn to navigate these risks and be safe online!

## The Golden Rules of Internet Safety

### 🔒 Rule 1: Protect Your Personal Information
**What is personal information?**
- Your full name, address, phone number
- Your school name and location
- Your parents' information
- Your passwords
- Photos that show where you live
- Your exact location
- Your birthday and age
- Your schedule and routines

**Why it matters:**
- Strangers could use this information to find you
- Scammers could use it to trick you or your family
- It could put you in danger

**What to do:**
✅ Ask a parent before sharing personal information
✅ Use privacy settings on all your accounts
✅ Think: "Would I give this information to a stranger on the street?" If no, don't share it online
✅ Use a username that doesn't include your real name

### 🤝 Rule 2: Be Smart About Online Friends

**Remember:**
- Not everyone online is who they say they are
- Some adults pretend to be kids to trick you
- Real friends respect your boundaries

**Warning signs of someone who might not be safe:**
🚩 They ask you to keep your friendship secret
🚩 They want to know personal details about you
🚩 They ask for photos or videos
🚩 They want to meet you in person
🚩 They ask you to do things that make you uncomfortable
🚩 They give you gifts or money
🚩 They get angry if you don't respond right away
🚩 They ask you not to tell your parents

**What to do:**
✅ Only accept friend requests from people you know in real life
✅ Tell a parent or trusted adult if someone makes you uncomfortable
✅ Block and report suspicious accounts
✅ Never agree to meet someone you only know online without your parents

### 💬 Rule 3: Think Before You Post

**Remember: The internet is FOREVER**
- Screenshots can save anything you post
- Deleted posts might still exist somewhere
- Future employers, teachers, or schools might see it
- It can affect your reputation for years

**Before posting, ask yourself:**
1. Would I be okay if my parents/teachers saw this?
2. Could this hurt someone's feelings?
3. Am I sharing too much personal information?
4. Will I be proud of this in 5 years?
5. Is this true and accurate?

**If the answer to any question is "no," DON'T POST IT.**

### 🛡️ Rule 4: Use Strong Passwords

**What makes a strong password?**
✅ At least 8-12 characters long
✅ Mix of uppercase and lowercase letters
✅ Includes numbers
✅ Includes symbols (!@#$%^&*)
✅ Not related to your personal information
✅ Different for each account

**Password don'ts:**
❌ Don't use your name, birthday, or pet's name
❌ Don't use "password" or "12345678"
❌ Don't share passwords with friends (except parents)
❌ Don't write passwords where others can see them

**Pro tip:** Use a passphrase! Example: "ILove2PlayBasketball!" is strong and easy to remember.

### 👀 Rule 5: Be Privacy-Smart

**Check your privacy settings:**
- Who can see your posts?
- Who can see your photos?
- Who can find you in searches?
- Who can see your location?
- Who can see your friend list?
- Who can message you?

**Best practice:**
- Set everything to "Friends Only" or more private
- Turn off location sharing
- Review settings every few months
- Ask a parent to help check settings

### 🤐 Rule 6: Don't Fall for Scams

**Common online scams targeting youth:**
- "You've won a prize! Click here!"
- "Free V-Bucks/Robux! Just enter your password!"
- Fake apps that look like real ones
- Messages claiming to be from a friend in trouble
- Too-good-to-be-true offers

**How to spot a scam:**
🚩 Asks for passwords or payment information
🚩 Has spelling or grammar mistakes
🚩 Creates urgency ("Act now!" "Limited time!")
🚩 Sounds too good to be true
🚩 From someone you don't know

**What to do:**
✅ Don't click suspicious links
✅ Don't download apps from unofficial sources
✅ Ask a parent before entering payment information
✅ Report scam messages

## Platform-Specific Safety Tips

### Social Media (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, etc.)
- Set your account to private
- Only accept followers you know
- Don't share your location in real-time
- Be careful with livestreaming
- Report bullying or inappropriate content
- Take breaks from social media when needed

### Gaming Platforms (Roblox, Minecraft, Fortnite, etc.)
- Use privacy settings to control who can communicate with you
- Don't share personal information in chat
- Report toxic players
- Don't click links in chat (could be scams or viruses)
- Be wary of "free items" offers
- Tell parents about in-game purchases

### Messaging Apps (WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, etc.)
- Only chat with people you know
- Don't join groups with strangers
- Be careful about what you share in groups
- Report inappropriate messages
- Don't share your phone number publicly

### Video Platforms (YouTube, Twitch, etc.)
- Be careful about what you reveal in videos or comments
- Don't meet people you only know from videos
- Report inappropriate content
- Remember that comments are public and permanent

## What to Do If Something Goes Wrong

### If Someone Makes You Uncomfortable Online
1. Stop communicating with them immediately
2. Don't delete messages (they're evidence)
3. Take screenshots if possible
4. Block the person
5. Tell a parent or trusted adult
6. Report to the platform

### If You're Being Cyberbullied
**Cyberbullying is:** Repeated, mean behavior online that hurts you

1. Don't respond or retaliate
2. Save evidence (screenshots)
3. Block the bully
4. Tell a parent, teacher, or trusted adult
5. Report to the platform and/or school
6. Remember: It's not your fault

**You deserve to feel safe online!**

### If You See Something Inappropriate
- Don't share it or forward it
- Report it to the platform
- Tell an adult
- Don't engage with the content

### If You Made a Mistake
**Everyone makes mistakes! Here's what to do:**
1. Tell a parent or trusted adult
2. Delete the content if possible
3. Learn from it
4. Don't panic - most things can be fixed
5. Ask for help to prevent it from happening again

**Remember: You won't get in as much trouble if you tell the truth and ask for help right away!**

## Your Rights Online

You have the right to:
✅ Feel safe online
✅ Privacy
✅ Be treated with respect
✅ Report abuse or harassment
✅ Take breaks from technology
✅ Say "no" to things that make you uncomfortable
✅ Ask for help from adults

## Digital Wellbeing Tips

### Healthy Tech Habits
- Take regular breaks (20-20-20 rule: Every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds)
- Don't use devices right before bed
- Have tech-free times (meals, homework, family time)
- Balance online and offline activities
- Notice how social media makes you feel

### Signs You Might Need a Break
- Feeling anxious or sad after being online
- Comparing yourself to others constantly
- Losing sleep to be online
- Ignoring friends/family to be on devices
- Getting angry when you can't be online

**It's okay to take a break or ask for help managing screen time!**

## Being a Good Digital Citizen

### Be KIND Online
- Treat others how you want to be treated
- Think before you comment
- Stand up for others being bullied
- Spread positivity, not negativity
- Give credit when sharing others' work

### Be SMART Online
- Check facts before sharing information
- Respect copyright and intellectual property
- Understand that not everything online is true
- Be critical of what you see and read

### Be RESPONSIBLE Online
- Your actions have consequences
- What you do online affects your real life
- Set a good example for younger kids
- Help others stay safe
