# Community Ambassador Training Program

Welcome to the Digital Guardians Community Ambassador Program! As an ambassador, you'll play a vital role in spreading digital safety awareness and empowering families in your community.

## What is a Community Ambassador?

Community Ambassadors are trained volunteers who:
- Conduct digital safety workshops in their communities
- Mentor families and share resources
- Raise awareness about online safety
- Build grassroots support for digital literacy
- Serve as trusted local resources

**You don't need to be a tech expert!** We'll provide training and all the resources you need.

## Ambassador Requirements

### Eligibility
✅ 18 years or older
✅ Passionate about digital safety
✅ Good communication skills
✅ Commitment to the program (minimum 6 months)
✅ Ability to conduct at least 2 workshops per quarter
✅ Completion of background check (for child safety)
✅ Agreement to Code of Conduct

### Time Commitment
- **Initial Training:** 8-12 hours (can be spread over 2-4 weeks)
- **Ongoing:** 8-10 hours per month on average
  - Workshop preparation and delivery
  - Community engagement
  - Continuing education
  - Ambassador meetings

## Training Program Structure

### Phase 1: Foundation Training (4-6 hours)

**Module 1: Digital Safety Fundamentals** (2 hours)
- Understanding digital risks and opportunities
- Core concepts of online safety
- Age-appropriate guidance
- Singapore's digital landscape
- Legal and ethical considerations

**Module 2: Communication and Education** (2 hours)
- Adult learning principles
- Engaging diverse audiences
- Cultural sensitivity
- Handling difficult questions
- Adapting content for different communities

**Module 3: Child Safeguarding** (2 hours)
- Recognizing signs of online harm
- Mandatory reporting requirements
- Professional boundaries
- Trauma-informed approach
- Singapore's child protection framework

### Phase 2: Practical Skills (4-6 hours)

**Module 4: Workshop Facilitation** (2 hours)
- Planning and preparing workshops
- Facilitation techniques
- Managing group dynamics
- Using visual aids and activities
- Time management

**Module 5: Resource Navigation** (1 hour)
- Digital Guardians resource library
- Creating your own materials
- Adapting existing resources
- Staying current with technology
- Where to get help

**Module 6: Community Engagement** (2 hours)
- Building partnerships (schools, community centers, etc.)
- Promoting your workshops
- Reaching underserved communities
- Multilingual outreach
- Follow-up and impact measurement

**Module 7: Practice Workshop** (1 hour)
- Deliver a mini-workshop to fellow trainees
- Receive feedback
- Refine your approach

### Phase 3: Specialization (Optional, 2-4 hours)

Choose one or more areas:
- **Parents of Young Children** (Ages 0-9)
- **Parents of Teens** (Ages 10-18)
- **Senior Citizens and Digital Literacy**
- **Multilingual Communities**
- **Special Needs Families**
- **School-Based Programs**

## Workshop Templates for Ambassadors

### Template 1: Parent Workshop - "Keeping Kids Safe Online" (90 minutes)

**Target Audience:** Parents of children ages 5-12

**Workshop Outline:**
1. **Welcome and Icebreaker** (10 min)
   - Introductions
   - Share one digital safety concern

2. **Understanding the Digital Landscape** (15 min)
   - What kids are doing online
   - Common platforms and apps
   - Age-appropriate activities

3. **Key Risks and How to Address Them** (20 min)
   - Inappropriate content
   - Online predators
   - Cyberbullying
   - Privacy concerns

4. **Practical Tools for Parents** (25 min)
   - Parental controls demonstration
   - Privacy settings walkthrough
   - Conversation starters
   - Family agreement template

5. **Interactive Activity** (15 min)
   - Small group discussions: scenario-based
   - Share solutions and strategies

6. **Q&A and Resources** (5 min)
   - Answer questions
   - Distribute resources
   - Follow-up information

**Materials Needed:**
- PowerPoint presentation (provided)
- Handouts (provided)
- Device for demonstrations
- Sign-in sheet
- Feedback forms

### Template 2: School Workshop - "Digital Citizenship for Youth" (45 minutes)

**Target Audience:** Secondary school students

**Workshop Outline:**
1. **Engage** (5 min)
   - Poll: Digital habits
   - Quick discussion

2. **Core Content** (20 min)
   - Digital footprint concept
   - Privacy and personal information
   - Cyberbullying awareness
   - Being a good digital citizen

3. **Interactive Activity** (15 min)
   - Case studies
   - Small group problem-solving
   - Role-playing scenarios

4. **Wrap-up** (5 min)
   - Key takeaways
   - Resources for help
   - Challenge: Practice one new skill this week

**Materials Needed:**
- Presentation slides (provided)
- Scenario cards (provided)
- Student handouts
- Posters

### Template 3: Community Awareness Session - "Digital Safety 101" (60 minutes)

**Target Audience:** General community members

**Workshop Outline:**
1. **Introduction** (10 min)
   - What is digital safety?
   - Why it matters to everyone

2. **Topics Overview** (30 min)
   - Protecting personal information
   - Recognizing scams
   - Social media safety
   - Cybersecurity basics
   - Where to get help

3. **Hands-On Practice** (15 min)
   - Check privacy settings
   - Create strong passwords
   - Identify phishing emails

4. **Resources and Next Steps** (5 min)
   - Take-home materials
   - Additional workshops
   - Contact information

## Ambassador Code of Ethics

As a Digital Guardians Ambassador, I commit to:

✅ **Act with Integrity**
- Provide accurate, evidence-based information
- Admit when I don't know something
- Refer to experts when appropriate

✅ **Respect Confidentiality**
- Protect privacy of workshop participants
- Handle sensitive information appropriately
- Follow data protection guidelines

✅ **Maintain Professional Boundaries**
- Keep appropriate distance in relationships
- Don't provide therapy or counseling
- Know when to refer to professionals

✅ **Promote Inclusivity**
- Welcome all community members
- Adapt for different needs and backgrounds
- Use inclusive language
- Challenge discrimination

✅ **Prioritize Child Safety**
- Follow safeguarding protocols
- Report concerns appropriately
- Never be alone with children
- Maintain professional conduct

✅ **Represent Digital Guardians Positively**
- Follow brand guidelines
- Communicate program values
- Maintain professional conduct
- Seek approval for media appearances

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need to be a tech expert?**
A: No! We provide all the training and resources. Your role is to facilitate learning and connect people with resources.

**Q: What if someone asks a question I can't answer?**
A: It's okay to say "I don't know." You can offer to find out and follow up, or refer them to expert resources.

**Q: Can I conduct workshops in languages other than English?**
A: Yes! We have materials in Mandarin, Malay, and Tamil. Additional languages can be developed.

**Q: How do I find venues for workshops?**
A: We'll help you connect with community centers, schools, and libraries. Many partners are already interested.

**Q: Is there a cost to be an ambassador?**
A: No! The program is free, and all materials are provided. You just bring your time and passion.

**Q: What if I need to take a break?**
A: Life happens! Just communicate with the program coordinator. You can pause and return when ready.
